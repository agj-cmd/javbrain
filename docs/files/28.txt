Stuffed Cabbage Rolls
Recipe, 🟢good
Ingredients: [
	• 2/3 cup water
	• 1 (10.5-ounce) can condensed tomato soup, divided
	• 8 large cabbage leaves
	• 1/4 cup chopped onion
	• 1/3 cup uncooked white rice
	• 1 pound lean ground beef
	• 1 large egg, slightly beaten
	• 1 teaspoon salt
	• 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
]
Instructions: [
	1. Prepare the Rice: [
		• In a saucepan, bring ⅔ cup water to a boil.
		• Stir in ⅓ cup uncooked white rice.
		• Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer until rice is tender and water is absorbed, approximately 20 minutes.
	]
	2. Prepare the Cabbage Leaves: [
		• Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil.
		• Carefully add 8 cabbage leaves and cook for 2 to 4 minutes until softened.
		• Drain and set aside.
	]
	3. Prepare the Filling: [
		• In a large bowl, combine the cooked rice, 1 pound lean ground beef, ¼ cup chopped onion, 1 slightly beaten egg, 2 tablespoons of the condensed tomato soup, 1 teaspoon salt, and ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper.
		• Mix thoroughly until well combined.
	]
	4. Assemble the Cabbage Rolls: [
		• Place a cabbage leaf on a flat surface.
		• Spoon approximately 2 tablespoons of the meat mixture onto the center of each leaf.
		• Fold in the sides and roll the leaf to enclose the filling.
		• Repeat with the remaining leaves and filling.
	]
	5. Cook the Cabbage Rolls: [
		• In a large skillet over medium heat, arrange the cabbage rolls seam-side down.
		• Pour the remaining condensed tomato soup over the rolls.
		• Cover and bring to a simmer.
		• Reduce heat to low and cook for about 40 minutes, until the meat is cooked through and the cabbage is tender.
	]
	Additional Tips: [
		• Alternative Method for Softening Cabbage Leaves: Instead of boiling, you can freeze the entire head of cabbage and then thaw it. This process makes the leaves tender and easy to work with, eliminating the need for boiling.
		• Variations: Feel free to customize the filling by adding herbs, spices, or substituting the ground beef with other proteins like ground pork, turkey, or lamb.
	]
]

Pozole
Recipe, mexican, soup, good, favorite
**Ingredients:** [
	- 3 lb pork loin or 4 lb bone-in pork shoulder, cut into 4 pieces
	- Salt and pepper to taste
	- 4 tbsp avocado oil, divided
	- 8 cups chicken broth
	- 2 bay leaves
	- 2 tsp ground cumin
	- 2 tsp dried oregano
	- 8 tomatillos, husks removed and halved
	- 3 poblano peppers, stems and seeds removed, halved
	- 2 jalapeño peppers, stems and seeds removed, halved
	- 1 large onion, quartered
	- 6 garlic cloves, peeled
	- 1 cup cilantro leaves and stems, tightly packed
	- 25 oz can of hominy, strained and rinsed
	- Shredded cabbage
	- Radish slices
	- Diced avocados
	- Diced onion
	- Cilantro
	- Cotija cheese
	- Lime wedges
	- Jalapeño slices
	- Tortilla chips
]
**Instructions:** [
	1. **Preheat Oven:** Set oven to 425°F (220°C).
	2. **Season and Sear Pork:** Generously season pork with salt and pepper. In a large Dutch oven or stockpot, heat 2 tbsp of oil over medium-high heat. Sear pork pieces on all sides until golden brown.
	3. **Simmer Pork:** Add chicken broth, bay leaves, cumin, and oregano to the pot. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 1 hour.
	4. **Roast Vegetables:** On a baking sheet, combine tomatillos, poblano peppers, jalapeños, onion, and garlic. Toss with remaining 2 tbsp of oil, salt, and pepper. Roast in the oven for 20 minutes until softened.
	5. **Combine and Cook:** Pour the blended salsa verde into the pot with the pork. Continue to simmer for an additional 2 hours, or until the pork is tender and shreds easily.
	6. **Prepare Salsa Verde:** Allow roasted vegetables to cool for 10 minutes. Transfer them, along with their juices, to a blender. Add cilantro and 1 cup of the pork broth from the pot. Blend until smooth.
	7. **Shred Pork:** Remove bay leaves and discard. Transfer pork pieces to a bowl, shred the meat, and discard any bones. Return shredded pork to the pot.
	8. **Add Hominy:** Stir in the rinsed hominy. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed.
	9. **Serve:** Ladle the pozole into bowls and garnish with your choice of toppings.
]

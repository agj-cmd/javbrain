Turkish Pasta (Deconstructed Manti) [
	Notes [
		• Seasoned ground beef over pasta with garlic yogurt and spiced butter sauce.
		• **Prep:** 15 min
		• **Cook:** 30 min
		• **Total:** 45 min
		• **Servings:** 4
		- Sub ground turkey for beef.
		- No pepper paste? Use 1 tbsp tomato paste in butter sauce instead.
		- For traditional flavor, add 1 tsp cumin with the beef seasonings.
		- Use 1 garlic clove for lighter flavor.
		- Use high-protein pasta to reduce carbs.
	]
	Ingredients [
		Pasta & Beef [
			- 8 oz short pasta (shells, elbows, bow ties)
			- 1 lb lean ground beef
			- 2 medium yellow onions, diced
			- 1 tbsp tomato paste
			- 2 tbsp onion powder
			- 2 tsp sweet paprika
			- Salt and pepper to taste
		]
		Butter Sauce [
			- ¼ cup unsalted butter
			- 2 tbsp olive oil
			- 1 tbsp mild pepper paste
			- 1 tbsp sweet paprika
			- 1 tsp dried mint
			- 1 tsp chili flakes
		]
		Yogurt Sauce [
			- 1 cup Greek yogurt
			- 2–3 garlic cloves, minced
			- Salt and pepper to taste
			- 2–3 tbsp water (to thin)
		]
		Garnish [
			- ¼ cup parsley, minced
			- ½ cup cherry tomatoes, diced
		]
	]
	Instructions [
		1. **Cook pasta** per package directions. Drain and set aside.
		2. **Brown beef** in a large skillet over medium heat, 2–3 min. No oil needed.
		3. **Season beef** — add onions, tomato paste, paprika, onion powder, salt, and pepper. Cook until onions are translucent, ~3 min. Remove from heat.
		4. **Butter sauce** — melt butter over low heat. Add olive oil, pepper paste, mint, paprika, and chili flakes. Stir 1 min. Set aside.
		5. **Yogurt sauce** — combine yogurt, garlic, salt, pepper, and water. Mix well.
		6. **Assemble** — plate pasta, spoon yogurt sauce over top, add beef, drizzle butter sauce, garnish with parsley and tomatoes.
	]
]

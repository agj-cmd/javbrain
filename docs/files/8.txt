# BBQ Ribs—Pressure Cooker

## Ingredients

### For the ribs:
- 2 racks baby back ribs (about 1.5–2 lbs each)

### For the dry rub:
- 2 tablespoons smoked paprika
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- 1.5 tablespoons chili powder
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

### For the BBQ sauce:
- 1.5 cups ketchup
- 3 tablespoons brown sugar
- 3 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
- 3 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- 1 teaspoon dry mustard
- 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 tablespoon vegetable or olive oil
- 1 large onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced or pressed

## Instructions

### Prepare the ribs:
- Line a large baking sheet or cutting board with foil for easy cleanup.
- Cut the ribs into 2-rib sections and place them on the prepared foil.

### Apply the dry rub:
- In a bowl, combine all dry rub ingredients.
- Sprinkle the rub evenly over both sides of the rib sections, massaging it into the meat.

### Prepare the BBQ sauce:
- In a mixing bowl, whisk together ketchup, 3 tablespoons brown sugar, Worcestershire sauce, apple cider vinegar, dry mustard, and 3/4 teaspoon salt.
- Set your pressure cooker to the sauté function. Add oil and diced onion, sautéing for 3–4 minutes until softened.
- Add minced garlic and cook for an additional 30 seconds, stirring frequently.
- Pour in the ketchup mixture, stirring to combine.

### Cook the ribs:
- Place a few rib sections into the pressure cooker at a time, turning them to coat with the sauce. Arrange the coated ribs upright in the pot, meat-side facing outward.
- Secure the lid and set the pressure cooker to high pressure for 30 minutes.
- Once cooking is complete, allow the pressure to release naturally for 15 minutes, then manually release any remaining pressure.

### Finish the sauce:
- Carefully remove the ribs from the pressure cooker and set aside.
- Activate the sauté function again and simmer the remaining sauce for about 10 minutes to thicken, if necessary.

### Optional broiling:
- For a charred exterior, preheat your oven's broiler.
- Place the ribs on a baking sheet, meat-side up, and broil for a few minutes until the desired level of caramelization is achieved.

### Serve:
- Generously brush the ribs with the thickened BBQ sauce.
- Serve any extra sauce on the side.

Poached Chicken with Scallion Ginger Sauce [
	Tags [
		 Recipe. Good. Asian.
	]
	About [
		 **Prep:** 20 min | **Cook:** 15 min | **Total:** 35 min
		 7-ingredient poached chicken. Make ahead, serve room temperature. Sauce similar to Cantonese steamed fish.
	]
	Ingredients [
		Chicken [
			- 5 chicken drumsticks (or 4 thighs; organic/kosher/free-range preferred)
			- 3 slices ginger
			- 1 scallion
		]
		Sauce [
			- 3 tbsp vegetable oil
			- 3 scallions (whites and greens separated, greens chopped)
			- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
			- 1/2 tsp sugar
			- 1/2 cup cooking water
			- 15g ginger (1.5 tbsp minced)
		]
	]
	Instructions [
		1. Boil 4 cups water with 3 ginger slices and 1 scallion
		2. Add chicken, return to boil
		3. Reduce to low heat, cover, simmer 10 min (water moving, no rolling boil)
		4. Turn off heat, leave covered 15 min
		5. Ice bath chicken 5 min, then shred onto plate
		6. Heat 3 tbsp oil on medium-low, fry scallion whites until crisp/brown
		7. Place fried scallion whites on chicken
		8. Add soy sauce, sugar, 1/2 cup cooking water to remaining oil, boil
		9. Add ginger and scallion greens, adjust seasoning
		10. Boil again, pour over chicken
		 **Note:** For boneless skinless breast: 5 min simmer, still 15 min off-heat. Test doneness: clear juices from thickest part.
	]
]

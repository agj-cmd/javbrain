Mug Brownie Chocolate Peanut Butter [
	Tags [
		Recipe
	]
	Ingredients [
		•  1/4 cup all-purpose flour
		•  1/4 cup granulated sugar
		•  2 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
		•  Pinch of salt
		•  2 tablespoons vegetable oil (or melted butter)
		•  3 tablespoons water
		•  Optional: 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract
		•  Optional toppings: chocolate chips, nuts, or ice cream
	]
	**Instructions:** [
		•  **Mix Dry Ingredients:** In a microwave-safe mug (at least 12 ounces), combine the flour, sugar, cocoa powder, and a pinch of salt. Stir them together until well mixed.
		•  **Add Wet Ingredients:** Add the vegetable oil and water to the dry ingredients. If using, also add the vanilla extract. Mix everything until smooth, ensuring there are no dry pockets at the bottom of the mug.
		•  **Add Extras:** If you like, stir in some chocolate chips or chopped nuts for extra texture and flavor.
		•  **Microwave:** Microwave on high for about 90 seconds. Start checking at 60 seconds, as microwave strengths can vary, and continue in 10-second increments until the brownie is set to your liking. It should still be moist when done, not dry.
		•  **Serve:** Let the mug brownie cool for a couple of minutes as it will be very hot. You can top it with a scoop of ice cream or a drizzle of chocolate sauce for an extra indulgent treat.
	]
]

Korean Beef [
	About [
		**Prep:** 10 minutes | **Cook:** 10 minutes | **Servings:** 6
	]
	Notes [
				•  Use lean ground beef or substitute turkey/chicken
				•  Serve over rice (white, brown, jasmine, or cauliflower)
				•  Add cooked vegetables for fiber
				•  Verify amounts when using recipe multiplier
	]
	Ingredients [
				•  ½ cup soy sauce
				•  ⅓ cup brown sugar
				•  2 teaspoons sesame oil
				•  2 tablespoons rice vinegar
				•  2 ½ teaspoons corn starch
				•  2 pounds ground beef
				•  salt and pepper, to taste
				•  3 garlic cloves, minced
				•  1 ½ knob fresh ginger, peeled and minced
				•  ½ cup green onion, sliced
				•  sesame seeds (optional, for garnish)
	]
	Instructions [
				1. Heat large skillet over medium heat.
				2. Mix soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil, rice vinegar, and cornstarch in bowl. Set aside.
				3. Add ground beef to skillet. Season with salt and pepper. Cook 5-7 minutes until no pink remains. Drain excess fat.
				4. Add garlic and ginger. Cook 1 minute.
				5. Reduce heat to low. Add sauce. Stir until thickened and mixed with beef.
				6. Stir in green onions.
				7. Serve and garnish.
	]
]

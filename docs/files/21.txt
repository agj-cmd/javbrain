Homemade Hamburger Helper Recipe [
	Tags [
		 Recipe. Good.
	]
	Ingredients: [
		• 1 pound extra lean ground beef
		• 1/2 medium onion, chopped
		• 2 heaping tablespoons tomato paste
		• 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
		• 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
		• 3 cups beef broth
		• 1 cup uncooked elbow macaroni
		• 2 cups freshly grated cheddar cheese
		• Salt & pepper to taste
	]
	Instructions: [
		1. Add ground beef and onions to a soup pot. Cook over medium-high heat, breaking the meat up as it browns (7-10 minutes). Drain excess fat if needed.
		2. Stir in tomato paste, garlic powder, and chili powder.
		3. Add beef broth and bring to a boil. Stir in the macaroni.
		4. Reduce heat to a rapid simmer. Cook uncovered, stirring occasionally, until the pasta is tender and most of the liquid is absorbed (13-15 minutes).
		5. Grate cheddar cheese while the pasta cooks.
		6. Remove the pot from heat and stir in cheese. Season with salt & pepper to taste. Let sit for a few minutes to absorb flavors before serving.
	]
	Tips: [
		• Add extra broth (1/2 cup at a time) if needed to prevent sticking.
		• Customize seasonings or add vegetables like spinach or bell peppers.
		• Substitute ground turkey or chicken if desired.
	]
	Storage: [
		• Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for 3-4 days. Reheat on low heat with a splash of broth if needed. Not recommended for freezing due to pasta texture changes.
	]

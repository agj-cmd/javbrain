	Ingredients [
		•  1 can coconut milk
		        •  full fat
		•  5 tablespoons cornstarch
		        •  +|• tablespoon for firmness
		•  4 tablespoons sugar
		•  1/2 cup water
	]
	Steps [
		•  Mix cornstarch and water in a small bowl.
		•  combine coconut milk and sugar. Whisk together. whisking until it's about to boil. Medium heat.
		•  Pour in cornstarch mixture, whisking.
		•  Heat low, cooking for 10-15 minutes. Whisk frequently.
		•  Ready when much thicker and starts to pull away from the side of the saucepan.
		•  Pour into a greased pan.
		•  Let cool at room temperature for 15 minutes.
		•  Then cool in the fridge for 1-2 hours until set.
	]
